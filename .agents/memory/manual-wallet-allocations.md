---
name: Manual wallet allocations
description: User-approved accounting approach for Super Admin cash adjustments
---
The user chose to extend the existing ledger rather than replace accounting
with a separate wallet balance.

**Why:** Manual allocations must affect the same available funds used by trading
and withdrawals, without double-counting cash or claiming a provider payment
occurred. They are capital adjustments, not trading profit.

**How to apply:** Preserve one accounting authority for client, admin, execution,
and withdrawal views. Require explicit authorized, audited money movements;
never seed financial balances merely to make a dashboard appear populated.
